About onlymove.net

This project was created by Henry Hemming and started on July 12th, 2025.

Problem Details

  • The ruleset is Japanese, with a 6.5 point komi.
  • Problems are generated by KataGo during self-play with marginally suboptimal moves.
  • To ensure quality, each problem is checked by the KataGo model kata9x9-b18c384nbt-20231025. The model uses 1,000 playouts to verify there is only a single winning move.
  • The collection contains over 90,000 unique problems. Of these, 65,000 are spread evenly across the ranks, while the rest are concentrated at the 20 kyu and 9 dan levels. Each problem can be displayed in any of eight orientations.
  • Problems are assigned a rating by having 60 different KataGo models attempt to solve them. These include b18c384nbt-humanv0 and kata9x9-b18c384nbt-20231025.
  • This process provides a rating for both the models and the problems. The system is anchored by aligning the b18c384nbt-humanv0 model's 3-dan rating to the 3-dan rank, and a similar anchor is used for the 8 kyu rank.

Ranking System

  • Your rank changes quickly at first, but the progression slows with each problem you fail.
  • Once your rank stabilizes, it will take roughly 10 wins to go up a rank or 10 losses to go down one.
  • Even high rank problems can be guessed correctly, but not frequently enough (4/5) to maintain your rank.
  • To help you learn, failed problems are shown again later using a spaced repetition system: first after 30-40 problems, and a second time after 180-250 problems.
  • Answering problems that are from links and not part of your own progression does not affect your rank.

Features

  • It can be installed from your browser as a standalone app.
  • The review mode has numerous positions pre-evaluated by kata9x9-b18c384nbt-20231025 with a minimum of 30 playouts, usually hundreds of playouts.
  • For positions that have not been pre-evaluated, it uses a server to provide kata9x9-b18c384nbt-20231025 with 1 playout to analyze positions.
  • Upon failure, a slow red screen will appear if you played too quickly (under 15 seconds) or if you have failed that specific problem before.
  • The statistics page tracks factors influencing your problem-solving probability. Ideally, time-related metrics should be close to zero, while a positive trend in the "per problem solved" metric indicates learning.
