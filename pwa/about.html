About onlymove.net

Created by Henry Hemming.

Problem Details

  • The ruleset is Chinese, with a 7.5 point komi.
  • Problems are generated by KataGo during self-play where it has been prompted to make suboptimal moves.
  • Each problem is then checked by the KataGo model kata1-b18c384nbt-s9996604416-d4316597426 (13617) with 100 playouts to confirm there is only one winning move.

Ranking System

  • Problem ranks are determined by having 600 different KataGo models attempt to solve them. Their models have been intentionally scrambled to make them weaker.
  • For users, ranks change quickly at first, it slows down with each failure.
  • Once your rank stabilizes, it will take roughly 10 wins to go up a rank or 10 losses to go down one.
  • Even high rank problems can be guessed correctly, but not frequently enough (4/5) to maintain your rank.

Features

  • It can be installed from your browser as a standalone app.
  • The review mode uses the KataGo model kata1-b15c192-s1672170752-d466197061 (elo 12213) with 10 playouts to analyze positions.
  • The uncomfortable failure animation is an intentional design choice to discourage low-effort attempts.
