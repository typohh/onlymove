About onlymove.net

Created by Henry Hemming, project started July 12th 2025.

Problem Details

  • The ruleset is Japanese, with a 6.5 point komi.
  • Problems are generated by KataGo during self-play with marginally suboptimal moves.
  • Each problem is then checked by the KataGo model kata9x9-b18c384nbt-20231025 with 1000 playouts separately on winning and losing moves to confirm there is only one winning move.
  • There are currently 41k unique problems, somewhat evenly spread out over the ranks. Each problem may be displayed in any of the 8 orientations.

Ranking System

  • Problem ranks are determined by having 60 different KataGo models attempt to solve them. These include b18c384nbt-humanv0, kata9x9-b18c384nbt-20231025.
  • For users, ranks change quickly at first, it slows down with each failure.
  • Once your rank stabilizes, it will take roughly 10 wins to go up a rank or 10 losses to go down one.
  • Even high rank problems can be guessed correctly, but not frequently enough (4/5) to maintain your rank.
  • Clicking on a rank gives more details, including do you remember correct answer to tsumegos you have seen recently or long time ago.
  • Any failed problem are shown twice again later, first after 30-40 tsumegos and second time after 180-250 tsumegos.
  • Answering problems that are from links and not part of your own progression does not affect your rank.
  • Only positions you have not seen before can increase your rank.

Features

  • It can be installed from your browser as a standalone app.
  • The review mode has numerous positions pre evaluated by kata9x9-b18c384nbt-20231025 with a minimum of 30 playouts, usually hundreds of playouts.
  • For positions that have not been pre evaluated, it uses server to provide kata9x9-b18c384nbt-20231025 with 1 playout to analyze positions.
  • The slow red screen appears when you fail, if either you played too fast (less than 15 seconds) or you had already been shown the problem previously.
