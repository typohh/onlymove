Privacy Policy

This policy covers onlymove.net and its installable app (including the Android app on Google Play, which is the same web app in a native wrapper). The short version: there are no user accounts in the traditional sense, no names, no email addresses, no advertising, and no third-party analytics. The app stores your solving history under a random account code so your progress can sync between your devices.

Effective date: July 13, 2026.

Your account code

  • When you first use the app, it generates a random account code on your device. This code is the only identifier attached to your data — we never ask for or store your name, email address, or any other personal details.
  • The code is shown under the Account menu. Entering it on another device links that device to the same solving history.
  • Treat the code like a password: anyone who has it can access and continue that solving history.

What we collect

Each time you attempt a problem, the app sends the result to our server so your progress is preserved and synced across devices:

  • Per-attempt records: your account code, the problem's ID, whether you solved it, the move you played, solving time, time spent in review, board size, whether it was a repeat, and a timestamp.
  • Rank survey answers (optional): the app occasionally asks what rank you hold on other Go servers. Answering is voluntary; answers are used to calibrate the rating-to-rank curve for everyone.
  • Review reminders (optional): if you enable push notifications, we store your device's push subscription (a delivery address issued by your browser) and the schedule of problems due for review, so reminders can be delivered.
  • Share-link events: when you create or open a shared problem link, we record the problem, the account code, the referring page for opened links, and a country-level location derived from the network connection at our hosting provider's edge. IP addresses themselves are not stored.
  • Your attempts on problems opened from shared links are not recorded — they reach neither the server's attempt log nor your own solving history, and they never affect your rank.

What we don't collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment details.
  • No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, no third-party analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No precise location. Only the country-level value mentioned above, and only for share-link events.

How the data is used

  • Syncing your solving history, rank, and review schedule across your devices.
  • Estimating your rank and selecting problems suited to you.
  • Calibrating problem difficulty for everyone: each problem's rating is refined by how often players at different strengths actually solve it.
  • Anonymous, aggregated community statistics (the Community tab): activity, rank distribution, and time-usage charts computed over the past week. Nothing shown there can identify an individual player.
  • We do not sell data, and we do not share it with third parties beyond the hosting infrastructure described below.

Storage on your device

  • Your recent solving history, settings, and account code are stored locally in your browser's storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) so the app works offline and loads quickly.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing site data removes this local copy. Your history remains on the server under your account code, so entering the code again restores it.

Hosting and infrastructure

  • The service runs on Cloudflare (Workers and the D1 database). Like any web host, Cloudflare processes IP addresses and request metadata to deliver the site and to protect it from abuse; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Board positions you analyze in review mode may be sent to our own analysis server to be evaluated by KataGo. Positions contain no personal data.
  • Push notifications are delivered through your browser vendor's push service (e.g. Google's, for Chrome on Android).

Data retention and deletion

  • Attempt records are kept indefinitely — they are what your rank, review schedule, and the community difficulty calibration are built from.
  • Push subscriptions are deleted when you disable notifications, and expired or unreachable subscriptions are cleaned up automatically.
  • To have all data associated with your account code deleted, email typo.hh@gmail.com and include the code (Account menu). Since the code is the only identifier we hold, we cannot locate your data without it.

Children

The app is a Go puzzle trainer suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included — only the pseudonymous solving records described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the change will be noted in the changelog on the about page.
